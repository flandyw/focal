# Product context

Focal helps VCE students plan their study, log practice exams, review mistakes, and see how their work adds up over time. The same web and desktop interface works great on its own, with optional account sync, friends, reminders, and AI tools if you want them.

Make the next step obvious, keep estimates clearly separate from official VCAA/VTAC data, and make logging quick enough to do right after finishing a paper or study block. Keep local data safe and easy to export. Support keyboard and touch navigation, keep focus states visible, and always pair colour cues with clear text.

See [interface design](web-DESIGN.md) and the repository's [product context](../PRODUCT.md).
